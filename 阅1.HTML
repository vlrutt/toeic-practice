Passage 1: E-mail
To: All Office Staff
From: Linda Moore, Office Manager
Date: November 12
Subject: New Parking Rules

Starting Monday, November 16, the company parking lot will have new rules. All employees must register their vehicle information at the front desk. You need to fill out a short form and show your driver’s license. After registration, you will receive a parking sticker.

Cars without the sticker cannot park inside the main lot. They must use the free parking area behind the building. Visitors should park in the visitor spaces near the front entrance. Please do not park in the fire lane at any time. If you have questions, please send an e-mail to parking@northcoastcorp.com.
1. What is the main purpose of this e-mail?
A. To announce new parking rules
B. To cancel a staff meeting
C. To ask workers to buy new cars
D. To close the parking lot forever
答案：A。主旨题。邮件主题就是新停车规定，通知全体员工。
2. What must employees get to park in the main lot?
A. A new driver’s license
B. A parking sticker
C. A new company car
D. A visitor card
答案：B。细节题。登记之后发放停车贴；没有贴纸不能停主停车场。
3. Where should visitors park?
A. Behind the building
B. Near the front entrance
C. In the fire lane
D. On the street
答案：B。细节定位：访客停在前门附近的访客车位。
Passage 2: Notice
City Public Library
Winter Reading Program

The City Public Library invites children aged 6 to 12 to join our Winter Reading Program. The program runs from December 1 to December 31. Children who read five or more books will receive a free gift bag with bookmarks, a pencil box and a reading certificate.

You can sign up at the main desk starting November 25. Parents must fill out a registration form. All books must be checked out from our library. Record each book you read on a reading log. Return your log by December 31 to get your gift. Gifts cannot be mailed and must be picked up in person.
4. Who can join this program?
A. Adults over 18
B. Children 6–12 years old
C. Library workers only
D. High school students
答案：B。原文第一句：6 至 12 岁儿童可以参加。
5. What do participants need to do to get a gift bag?
A. Read at least five books
B. Work at the library
C. Buy new books
D. Bring a gift to the library
答案：A。读五本或者更多图书可以领取礼品袋。
6. How can you receive the gift?
A. It will be sent by mail
B. You must pick it up yourself
C. It will arrive at your school
D. You can download it online
答案：B。礼品不能邮寄，必须亲自领取。
Passage 3: Double Passage
【Memorandum】
To: All Sales Team Members
From: Robert King, Sales Director
Date: November 15
Subject: Sales Conference

Our yearly sales conference will be held on Friday, December 4, at the Downtown Business Center. The meeting will begin at 9:00 a.m. and end at 4:00 p.m. Please prepare a short report about your recent sales results. Each person will have five minutes to speak. Lunch will be provided at noon. If you cannot attend, please tell me by November 25.
【Reply E-mail】
To: Robert King
From: Susan Hill
Date: November 18

Dear Mr. King,
Thank you for your memo about the sales conference. I am happy to join the meeting on December 4. I am preparing my sales report and will finish it by the end of this week. I would like to know if I can bring one customer guest to the lunch. Please let me know whether this is allowed. Thank you very much.
Sincerely,
Susan Hill
7. When will the sales conference take place?
A. November 15
B. November 25
C. December 4
D. December 31
答案：C。备忘录写明：12 月 4 日周五举行销售大会。
8. What should each sales team member prepare?
A. A sales report
B. A new office suit
C. A lunch box
D. A conference ticket
答案：A。要求每人准备一份近期销售成果报告。
9. What is true about Susan Hill?
A. She cannot come to the conference
B. She plans to attend the conference
C. She is the sales director
D. She wants to change the date
答案：B。Susan 回信表示很高兴参加本次会议。
10. What does Susan ask Mr. King about?
A. The time of lunch
B. Whether she can bring a guest to lunch
C. The location of the center
D. How long her speech will be
答案：B。Susan 询问是否可以带一位客户来宾一起吃午餐。
