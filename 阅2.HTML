Passage 1: Notice
Greenfield Office Building Maintenance Notice

Please note that the central air-conditioning system in the Greenfield Office Building will receive maintenance on Thursday, November 26. The work will begin at 8:00 a.m. and finish at approximately 5:00 p.m. During this period, there will be no air conditioning in all office rooms. We suggest all staff bring light jackets. If the temperature becomes uncomfortable, you may use the portable fans available at the front desk on each floor. The maintenance team will try their best to finish the work early. If you have any emergency problems, please contact the property management office at ext. 307. We apologize for the inconvenience.
1. What is the notice mainly about?
A. A new air conditioner sale
B. Air-conditioning maintenance
C. Moving to a new office building
D. A staff holiday party
答案：B。主旨题。通知告知中央空调维修，维修期间暂停供冷供暖。
2. When will the maintenance work take place?
A. November 26
B. November 25
C. December 5
D. December 26
答案：A。原文第一句：维修时间是 11 月 26 日周四。
3. What can staff get at the front desk?
A. Free cold drinks
B. Portable fans
C. New jackets
D. Room keys
答案：B。每层前台提供便携式风扇供员工使用。
Passage 2: Customer E-mail
Dear Ms. Wilson,

Thank you for your order No. 7249 placed on November 10. We are sorry to inform you that two of the items you ordered are temporarily out of stock. Martin Lee
Star Office Supplies

B. To tell her some goods are not in stock
C. To thank her for a gift
D. To give her a free gift
答案：B。马丁写信告知有两件商品暂时缺货，提供两种发货方案。
5. Which items are unavailable right now?
A. Desk lamp and file cabinet
B. Pens and notebooks
C. Chairs and tables
D. Boxes and stickers
答案：A。黑色台灯和木质文件柜暂无库存。
6. What is true about the second option?

C. All items arrive on December 3

答案：B。分开发货不会收取额外运费。
