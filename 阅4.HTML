Passage 1: Notice
Bright Page Bookstore
Holiday Opening Notice

Please note our special opening hours during the December holiday season. From December 1 until December 24, we will open at 8:30 a.m. and close at 9:00 p.m. This is one hour longer than our normal hours. Our usual closing time is 8:00 p.m. We have many new books, calendars and gift sets for holiday shoppers. Customers who spend over $40 will receive a free cloth shopping bag. The free gift is available while supplies last. We will be closed all day on December 25 for Christmas. We reopen on December 26 at our regular time. Thank you for your support.
1. What is the purpose of this notice?
A. To announce special holiday hours
B. To cancel a book sale
C. To hire new shop workers
D. To change the bookstore address
答案：A。主旨题。通知公布节日期间调整后的营业时间以及福利活动。
2. When does the bookstore normally close?
A. At 8:00 p.m.
B. At 8:30 p.m.
C. At 9:00 p.m.
D. At 9:30 p.m.
答案：A。原文说明：平时关门时间是晚上八点。
3. What can customers get if they spend more than $40?
A. A free book
B. A cloth shopping bag
C. A free calendar
D. A holiday discount card
答案：B。消费超过 40 美元赠送帆布购物袋，送完即止。
Passage 2: Delivery E-mail
Dear Mr. Hoffman,

This message is about your package tracking number 67148. Your package was sent out yesterday morning and arrived at the local delivery center at 2:00 p.m. today. Our delivery driver plans to deliver it to your home this afternoon between 3:00 and 5:00 p.m. If no one is at home, the driver will leave a delivery notice on your front door. You can pick up your package at our delivery office or ask us to try again tomorrow. If you know you will not be home, please reply to this e-mail right away so we can change the delivery time. QuickShip Delivery
4. What is the main idea of this e-mail?
A. To ask Mr. Hoffman to buy a new package
B. To tell Mr. Hoffman about his package delivery
C. To return money to Mr. Hoffman
D. To close the delivery office
答案：B。快递邮件通知包裹派送进度以及派送时间安排。
5. When will the driver try to deliver the package?
A. Yesterday morning
B. This afternoon
C. Tomorrow morning
D. Tomorrow evening
答案：B。司机计划今天下午三点到五点上门派送。
6. What happens if nobody is home?
A. The package will be destroyed
B. A notice will be left at the door
C. The driver will wait for one hour
D. The package will go back to the sender right away
答案：B。家里无人时，司机会在门上留下取件通知单。
