Passage 1: City Notice
Westside Road Maintenance Notice

The city road department will repair part of Westside Road starting Monday, December 7. The work will last for five days, until Friday, December 11. Workers will fix broken road surfaces and paint new traffic lines. Between 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m., only one lane will be open. Drivers should expect slow traffic and short delays. Please try to use Maple Street as an alternate route. No parking is allowed along the repair area during working hours. Cars left there will be towed. We apologize for the trouble and thank you for your patience.
1. What is the notice mainly about?
A. A new road opening
B. Road repair work
C. A new bus schedule
D. A driving test
答案：B。主旨题。通知告知西路道路维修施工、交通管制以及绕行建议。
2. How long will the repair work continue?
A. One week
B. Five days
C. Two weeks
D. One month
答案：B。施工从 12 月 7 日到 11 日，一共五天。
3. Which street is suggested as an alternate route?
A. Maple Street
B. Westside Street
C. Oak Road
D. Central Avenue
答案：A。官方建议绕行 Maple Street。
